Jaundice(Gilbert Syndrome)

Jaundice: A Brief Overview

Jaundice is a condition characterized by the yellowing of the skin, sclera (the white part of the eyes), and mucous membranes due to an excess of bilirubin in the blood...

Physiology of Jaundice

Bilirubin is a waste product generated when red blood cells break down in the spleen. This bilirubin is initially in an unconjugated (indirect) form and is transported to the liver...

Types of Jaundice

Pre-hepatic (Hemolytic) Jaundice: This occurs due to the excessive breakdown of red blood cells, leading to a high production of unconjugated bilirubin...

Hepatic Jaundice: This occurs when the liver is damaged and cannot process bilirubin effectively. It can result from hepatitis, cirrhosis, liver cancer...

Post-hepatic (Obstructive) Jaundice: This occurs when there is an obstruction in the bile ducts, preventing conjugated bilirubin from being excreted into the intestines...

Causes of Jaundice

Jaundice can be caused by several factors, including liver diseases, hemolysis, obstruction of bile ducts, genetic disorders, infections, medications, and liver cancer...

Ayurvedic Treatment for Jaundice

In Ayurveda, jaundice is primarily considered a Pitta dosha disorder, with the accumulation of excess bile (pitta) causing the symptoms of jaundice. The liver is viewed as the organ responsible for digestion, detoxification, and regulation of bile...

Ayurvedic Treatment Approach for Jaundice

  • Dietary Modifications: Ayurveda emphasizes a light, easy-to-digest diet to reduce the load on the liver...
  • Hydration and Herbal Teas: Hydration is key in managing jaundice. Herbal teas made from coriander, ginger, or cumin can help improve digestion...
  • Lifestyle Modifications: Avoiding excessive heat, stress, and overexertion is important to prevent Pitta imbalance...

Conclusion

Jaundice is a condition that results from the accumulation of bilirubin in the blood due to impaired liver function or bile obstruction. In Ayurveda, jaundice is primarily treated by addressing Pitta imbalance, improving liver function, and detoxifying the body...
